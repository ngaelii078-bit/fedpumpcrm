# LexBridge Group CRM demo

The CRM follows the LexBridge Group website's navy and orange palette and uses its logo and interwoven strands as the visual identity. It is designed as an operations workspace for an independent logistics brokerage.

The dashboard retains the original CRM's responsive navigation, clear data hierarchy and reduced-motion support. Customers, staff, values and jobs are sample data. Messages are simulated and are never sent.
