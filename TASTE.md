# Fedpump CRM demo

skip bakeoff: approved by the owner, 25 Nov 2026 (picked "Skip bakeoff" when asked).
Direction was set by the reference instead: the "Sales CRM dashboard" reel by @_code_and_chill_.

## Intent
Opening it should feel like standing on Fedpump's own workshop floor after hours with every job under control.
Their blurred workshop photo is the light source, and every panel is glass in front of it.

## What carries over from the reel
Frosted glass on a blurred photo, centred pill nav with a lead and trail indicator, left tool rail,
two line page titles with inline figures, tinted pipeline columns, draggable deals, Won and Lost wells.

## What makes it Fedpump
- Signature move: drag the strip slider and the pump comes apart as a section drawing, each part marked reuse, recondition or replace.
- Six bolts. The logo is a six bolt flange and a job has six stages. Progress is bolts torqued in star order.
- Pipeline stages follow their real process: Enquiry, Strip & inspect, Quoted, PO pending.
- Customers are grouped by their four industries: Mining, Manufacturing, Agriculture, Water.
- Every customer carries a pump register, so the next refurbishment becomes a sales lead.

## Guardrails
- Fonts: Manrope (display), DM Sans (text), DM Mono (refs and measurements). First two are the fedpump.com families.
- One accent: gold #EDB771 on navy #0B1C2C. Casing orange and steel appear only as data or stage colour.
- Chart colours: #BF8632 #4A90D9 #E0603A #2FA37A #B070C4, validated for colour blindness and contrast on #1A2027.
- Easing power3.out. Only transform, opacity and clip-path animate. Reduced motion turns all of it off.
- No em dashes in copy. No 3D built from primitives. No photos of people.
- All customers, staff, values and jobs are invented. No message is ever sent from the demo.
